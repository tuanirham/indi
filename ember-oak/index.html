How your coffee gets this good

No warehouses, no middlemen, no mystery. Three steps between the farm gate and your grinder.

01

Sourced at origin

We buy directly from 24 smallholder farms across 9 countries, paying an average of 2.4× the fair-trade floor price — and we publish every contract.

02

Roasted in small batches

Batches of 12 kg max on our 1962 Probat drum, profiled by hand and cupped by three tasters before any bag is sealed. Reject rate last year: 11%.

03

Delivered within 48 hours

Orders placed before midnight ship the morning after roast day. In the Klang Valley, most bags arrive while they're still de-gassing.
