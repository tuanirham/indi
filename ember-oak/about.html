Built around one old drum
and a stubborn idea

The idea: coffee tastes better when everyone in the chain — farmer, roaster, barista, drinker — gets a fair share of the good part.

The beginning

A 1962 Probat, rescued from a warehouse in Penang

In 2014, Amirul and Mei Ling found a rusted 12-kilo Probat drum roaster in a shuttered kopitiam supply warehouse. Six months of weekends later, it ran again — and it has run every Tuesday and Thursday since.

We started by roasting for friends, then for one cafe, then for forty. But we kept the drum, and we kept the rule that came with it: no batch bigger than 12 kg. Small batches force you to pay attention. Attention is the whole craft.

Today a team of eleven sources, roasts, brews, and ships from Chow Kit — and every contract we sign at origin is published on this website, because "direct trade" should be checkable, not a vibe.

Twelve years, four milestones

The short version

2014

The drum comes home

Restored the Probat in a rented shoplot in Chow Kit. First roast: a Sumatra so uneven we composted the whole batch. Second roast: better.

2017

First origin contract

Signed directly with the Idido washing station in Yirgacheffe — our longest-running partnership, now in its ninth harvest.

2021

The roastery cafe opens

Knocked down the wall between the roastery and the street. Now you can watch your beans roast while you drink the previous batch.

2025

Carbon-neutral certified

Compostable packaging, solar on the roof, and sea freight only. Verified carbon-neutral across roasting and last-mile delivery.

What we won't compromise

Three house rules

Pay farmers properly

Average 2.4× the fair-trade floor, multi-year contracts, and pre-harvest financing. Every contract is published — check us.

Freshness is non-negotiable

Roast dates on every bag, 48-hour dispatch, and a 14-day freshness guarantee. Coffee is produce, not pantry stock.

Leave it better

Carbon-neutral operations, compostable bags, chaff to community gardens, and grounds collected back from our cafes for compost.
